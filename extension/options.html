Unofficial Google integration
01 / LOCAL VAULT

Protect your 2FA keys.

Choose a separate password for this extension. It encrypts your keys and Google access token on your device.

This password stays on your device. You need to unlock the vault after restarting Chrome or after 30 minutes without use.

Unlock Authenticator.

Enter your local vault password to access your accounts.

02 / GOOGLE ACCOUNT

Sync Authenticator.

Connecting allows the extension to read account names and secret 2FA keys from Google Authenticator and save them in your encrypted local vault. You complete sign-in on Google’s website.

Google sign-in is open in another tab. Complete it to see your accounts here.

Google did not provide your email automatically. Enter the address you just selected on Google to finish connecting.

03 / WEBSITE SUGGESTIONS

Your code beside the 2FA field.

Allow access to HTTPS websites to show suggestions at sign-in. A code is filled when you select an account.

Accounts are linked to an exact domain. For an unrecognized site, open the extension and choose “Link to this site” beside an account. You can grant access to that site only.

Codes are generated locally and also work offline.
